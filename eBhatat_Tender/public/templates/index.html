Guidelines

Role-Based Guidelines

Select your role to view the relevant procurement guidelines and compliance requirements.

Vendor Guidelines

For registered & prospective vendors

  • Register with valid business documents
  • Complete profile with GST, PAN & KYC
  • Read tender documents before bidding
  • Submit technical & financial bids on time

Department Guidelines

For government offices

  • Create tenders with clear specifications
  • Upload BOQ & technical documents
  • Set fair eligibility criteria
  • Evaluate bids strictly as per policy

Policy Guidelines

For authorities & policy enforcement

  • Ensure transparency in all procurement
  • Promote equal opportunity to all vendors
  • Prevent corruption and malpractice
  • Maintain complete digital audit records
